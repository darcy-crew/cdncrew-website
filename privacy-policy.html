Privacy
Policy

Last updated: 2025

CDN Crew is a trade name of 2359555 Alberta Inc. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information provided to us through cdncrew.com. We collect only what we need, we don't sell it, and we protect it.

Who we are

CDN Crew is a trade-only graphic installation company based in Calgary, Alberta, operating as a division of 2359555 Alberta Inc. Our website is cdncrew.com. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at office@cdncrew.com.

What information we collect

We collect information only when you choose to provide it — primarily through our quote request form. This may include your name and company name, your email address and phone number, project details you provide in the form, and your general location for the purposes of quoting. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use your information

Information submitted through our quote form is used solely to respond to your quote request, communicate with you about your project, and follow up on previous inquiries if relevant. We do not use your information for marketing without your consent, and we do not add you to mailing lists or automated email sequences.

Who we share your information with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Quote form submissions are processed through Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service. Their privacy policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous. We do not use advertising cookies or track individual users across the web.

How we protect your information

Our website is served over HTTPS, meaning data transmitted between your browser and our site is encrypted. Form submissions are routed through Formspree's secure infrastructure. We do not store personal data in any database connected to this website.

How long we keep your information

We retain project and contact information for as long as is reasonably necessary for business purposes — typically for the duration of a project relationship and a reasonable period thereafter. If you would like your information removed from our records, contact us and we will action it promptly.

Your rights

Under Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, request a correction to inaccurate information, and request that we stop using or delete your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at office@cdncrew.com. We will respond within 30 days.
