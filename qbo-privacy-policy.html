Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  2359555 Alberta Inc. (CDN Crew Installations)

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how CDN Crew Installations, a division of 2359555 Alberta Inc., handles data accessed through the CDN Crew Internal Automation application (the "Application").

The Application is an internal workflow tool operated exclusively by CDN Crew Installations for its own business operations. It is not available to the public, has no end-consumer users, and is not listed on any app marketplace.

Data We Access

The Application connects to CDN Crew's own QuickBooks Online account via the Intuit API. The following data is accessed solely for internal operational purposes:

  • Customer names and contact information (CDN Crew's existing trade clients)
  • Invoice records and service item names
  • Company financial data belonging to CDN Crew Installations

No personal consumer data is collected. All data accessed belongs to CDN Crew Installations or its direct trade clients, and is accessed only by authorized CDN Crew staff.

How Data Is Used

Data accessed through the Application is used exclusively to:

  • Automate internal invoicing and job tracking workflows
  • Maintain canonical reference data for job intake and quoting
  • Support internal business operations of CDN Crew Installations

Data Storage and Security

Data is processed within Google Workspace (Google Apps Script) under CDN Crew's existing Google account. No data is stored in external databases or third-party services beyond Google Workspace and QuickBooks Online, both of which CDN Crew subscribes to independently.

Access is limited to authorized CDN Crew staff accounts. Credentials are stored in Google Apps Script's encrypted Script Properties and are never exposed in source code or shared externally.

Third-Party Sharing

CDN Crew Installations does not share, sell, license, or disclose any data accessed through the Application to any third parties. All data remains within CDN Crew's internal systems (Google Workspace and QuickBooks Online).

Data Retention

Data accessed via the Application is not retained beyond the operational session in which it is used. Reference data written to Google Sheets (customer names, service item names) is maintained only as long as it is operationally relevant and is subject to CDN Crew's standard data management practices.

Your Rights

As this Application is for internal use only and processes data belonging to CDN Crew Installations, it is not subject to consumer privacy rights requests. Any CDN Crew staff questions about data handling may be directed to the contact below.

Changes to This Policy

CDN Crew Installations may update this Privacy Policy as the Application evolves. Changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
