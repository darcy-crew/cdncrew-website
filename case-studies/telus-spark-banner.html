Exterior Banner · Telus Spark Calgary

High-access exterior banner.
Active public venue.

Project type
Exterior Banner
Location
Telus Spark, Calgary
Access
High-access rigging
Environment
Active public venue

The project

A print partner needed a large exterior banner installed on the facade of the Telus Spark science centre in Calgary — a major public venue that operates daily and couldn't accommodate extended access disruptions. The banner required high-access rigging to position and secure at the correct height on the building face.

The challenge

  • High-access installation requiring specialist rigging equipment
  • Active public venue — installation had to work around public programming hours
  • Large format banner requiring precise positioning against the building facade
  • Exterior exposure requiring correct anchoring for wind load and long-term durability
  • Completion documentation required same day for the client's campaign launch

How we executed it

CDN Crew coordinated access timing with Telus Spark's facilities team to work within a window that didn't disrupt public programming. Equipment was staged efficiently to minimise the footprint in a public-facing area.

The banner was rigged, positioned, and secured with anchoring appropriate for exterior exposure in Calgary's weather conditions. Completion photos were sent to the print partner before the venue opened for its next programming session.

The Result

Exterior banner installed, secured, and documented within the access window. The venue's public programming was unaffected. The banner is visible on the building face at the correct position and height — exactly as specified in the installation brief.
