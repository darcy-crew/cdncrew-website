The project

A residential development marketing campaign required a 20-foot by 55-foot exterior graphic installed on the face of the Junction 88 building in Calgary. At those dimensions, this is a significant large-format exterior install — the kind of project that requires both technical precision and the right equipment for a building-scale application.

Residential developments use building graphics like this to establish presence in the market and communicate the brand and availability messaging to the surrounding community. Getting it right means the graphic reads clearly from the street, sits flat and secure against the building face, and looks professional for the months it will be in place.

The challenge

  • 20' × 55' graphic — large enough to require equipment and a coordinated install sequence
  • New construction environment — site coordination with the building's management team
  • Multi-panel application requiring precise joins at a scale visible from street level
  • Exterior mounting to withstand Calgary's wind and weather conditions
  • Installation documented for the developer's marketing team on completion

How we executed it

The graphic was installed in sections, working from the top down to ensure each panel was correctly positioned before the next was applied. At 55 feet wide, maintaining horizontal alignment across the full span of the graphic requires a disciplined installation sequence and continuous checking.

The final result is visible from the street — a clean, flat, professional building graphic that communicates the Junction 88 brand and availability messaging clearly to the surrounding neighbourhood.

The Result

A 20' × 55' building graphic installed flat, aligned, and secure. The developer's marketing team had a building presence that matched the quality of the brand — visible from the street and professionally executed from every angle.
