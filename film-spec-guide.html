Film Selection & Spec Guide

3M · Avery Dennison · Drytac · General Formulations · PhotoTex · Contra Vision

15+ yrs · 95% referral · Alberta

This guide is a working reference for the print shops, marketing agencies and installation partners we work with across Alberta. It distils 15+ years of field experience into a one-page film-selection tool — what we’d typically reach for given the substrate, the durability class, the environment, and Alberta’s shoulder-season temperatures. It covers the six product lines we see most often on real jobs (3M, Avery Dennison, Drytac, General Formulations, PhotoTex and Contra Vision) plus the prep specs and cold-install rules that keep installs from coming back. Use it to brief a client, sanity-check a quote, or shortcut a product conversation with your supplier.

This matrix is a starting point, not a complete specification. Manufacturers change formulations, discontinue lines and update bulletins regularly, and no single sheet can cover every substrate, climate or project condition. Always verify the current manufacturer’s product data sheets and instruction bulletins before ordering, and run an on-site adhesion test before quoting permanence. CDN Crew makes no warranty as to film selection, product performance, adhesion, or long-term durability — those sit with the manufacturer and the chosen product. What we do warranty is our installation workmanship, on every job we put our name on.

❄ Cold installs — below 8 °C (46 °F)
Standard pressure-sensitive vinyl bottoms out around 5–10 °C (41–50 °F), and Avery MPI 1105 needs ~15 °C. These are the films actually rated colder.
PhotoTex — the field favourite

Peel & Stick Fabric

  • Polyester fabric backing stays flexible in cold; vinyl gets brittle
  • Manufacturer rates it for extreme heat & extreme cold conditions
  • Class A fire · PVC & PFAS-free · LEED — clean install for sensitive interiors
  • Liquid-laminate for outdoor / longer durability (6–12 mo)
  • Use EX/EXS on stucco / eggshell / texture · original on smooth
Drytac — walls & windows

Polar Grip + ReTac

  • Polar Grip — exterior, to −20 °C / −4 °F (proven in BC winter)
  • ReTac Smooth / Textures — removable interior walls & windows, to 5 °C / 41 °F
Walls & signage

Continental Grafix X-Treme

  • Rated to −15 °C / +5 °F
  • High-tack calendered, bonds to LSE plastics & textured substrates
  • Up to 5-yr outdoor — extreme-cold workhorse
General Formulations

Concept 203

  • Rated to +4 °C / +40 °F
  • Calendered, flat smooth surfaces only
  • Budget cold option for hoarding / panels
Floors & walls

Continental Grafix Walk&Wall

  • Rated to −4 °C / +25 °F
  • Textured non-slip, no lamination needed
Perforated window

Cold-spec perf

  • Contra Vision to +4 °C / 39 °F; AutoGraph (overlaminated) to 0 °C / 32 °F
  • panoRama Innova Ice — 60/40, to −15 °C / +5 °F, no heat
Technique — applies to all

Stack the deck

  • Warm the substrate & store rolls warm until install
  • IPA mist on adhesive buys ~6 °C of headroom
  • Post-heat & re-roll every edge; watch dew point / condensation
  • Below the film’s min — walk. Failure shows up weeks later
Perforated window film — quick reference
One-way vision works while the inside is darker than outside, and reverses after dark (graphic disappears, interior visible). Set client expectations on night appearance.
Specialist range — Contra Vision

Pick by lifespan

  • Performance — premium white-on-black, up to 5 yr; transit, building & long-term storefront
  • Advance — PVC-free PET, up to 3 yr, lower carbon
  • Campaign — value range, up to 2 yr, short-term promo
  • Performance HD — micro-perf (1 mm holes), sharpest image & smoothest see-through
  • Clear — clear-backed for interior, keeps natural light & clean look
Application side

Interior vs exterior

  • Exterior-applied: white-face / black-back, graphics out — best outdoor visibility; laminate or edge-seal
  • Interior-applied: clear-backed (Contra Vision Clear), viewed from outside, protected from weather
  • Also: 3M Scotchcal Perforated · Avery MPI perforated · GF WindowMark (232/260/270/280)
Tint ratio (open area)

See-through vs image

  • 50/50 — balanced, common storefront
  • 60/40 — brighter graphic, less see-through
  • 70/30 · 80/20 — max image, lowest outward view
  • Winter install? Use a cold-spec perf — see cold panel
Production & prep spec
What we need from the print shop, and what we do on site, to kill the seam / edge / adhesion callbacks.
SpecStandardNotes
Bleed0.25″–0.5″ loose graphics · 0.75″–1″ wrapping a substrate edge · 0.125″ flush-trim mountedTell us trimmed vs. wrap-edge before printing.
Panel overlap (seams)0.5″ smooth walls · 1″ textured · butt / kiss-cut where seamless is neededSequence panels so overlap faces away from the main sightline — kills the shadow line.
Panel width / tilingPlan to material & printer width (commonly ≤54″)Place seams at logical breaks (corners, mullions), not across a face or logo.
Surface prepClean with 70% IPA / degreaser, dry fully, dust-freeDon’t use IPA on finished laminate faces (DOL-Z, high gloss) — it can matte them. PhotoTex Opaque: use damp rag only, not IPA.
Paint cureLatex fully cured — often 30+ days before any filmFresh paint is the #1 hidden adhesion failure (same for PhotoTex).
Adhesion testWalls: 3M MCS warranty needs ≥ 1000 g / 15 minTest painted walls before quoting permanence; leave a scrap up a few days if you can.
WindowsDecide first-surface (interior-applied) vs second-surface; allow for frame / muntin overlapPerf: interior clear-back vs exterior white/black — confirm view direction first.
FloorsClean & dry, no fresh wax / sealer, round the corners, tamp edgesSlip rating is a safety / liability item in public spaces — don’t skip it.
